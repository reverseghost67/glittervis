# Contributing to GlitterVis

GlitterVis is a personal project, shared as-is under the MIT license. It is **read-only**: pull requests and feature requests are not accepted.

**What is welcome:** a *Compatibility report* (Issues tab), telling me whether it runs on your PC and graphics card (and the frame rate from `glittervis.log`). Never include IP addresses, account names or file paths that identify you.

You are free to fork it and change it for your own use.
